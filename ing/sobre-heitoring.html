Municipality: Toledo - PR

  • History and Geography: Created in the 1950s from the settlement driven by the Maripá colonizing company and led mostly by migrants from Rio Grande do Sul and Santa Catarina, the municipality emancipated itself from Foz do Iguaçu in 1951 and became an agro-industrial hub. Considered the "Paraná Capital of Agribusiness", Toledo hosts one of the largest slaughterhouse complexes in Brazil, being the leader in the state's agricultural GDP.
  • Culture and Curiosities: Toledo is nationally known as the "Capital of Agribusiness" and famous for the traditional National Roasted Pork Festival. The city also has, right in the center, the Diva Paim Barth Ecological Park, one of the favorite places for people to enjoy their free time.

Neighborhood: Jardim Pancera

  • Local History: Jardim Pancera is one of the most populous neighborhoods in Toledo — it had about 3,227 residents in the 2010 Census, and more recent estimates speak of up to 6,110. Today the neighborhood is experiencing accelerated growth, with new subdivisions and population increase putting pressure on infrastructure — so much so that the local UBS (Basic Health Unit) entered renovation and expansion in 2025..
  • Characteristics and Remarkable Aspects:The central square, João Cirino dos Santos Sobrinho, was revitalized in 2019 and gained a Third Age Academy, Health Station and children's playground. The neighborhood crowd is also active: since at least 2013 they have been demanding improvements such as a synthetic football field, covered bus stops and lighting, and in 2014 they got a pre-molded shed attached to the Community Center, built through the Participatory Budget. And now the curiosity nobody expected: the square is named after João Cirino dos Santos Sobrinho, but there's no statue of him, no. Whoever got a bronze tribute there was... Vinicius de Moraes! The carioca poet, author of "Girl from Ipanema", has his monument planted in a square in Toledo, Paraná — while João Cirino himself, who gives the place its name, was left with just the naming itself.
