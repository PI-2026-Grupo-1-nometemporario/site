School Trajectory

Lucca was always a boy who liked to go to school to play, at least until the second stage of elementary school. He started studying at Escola Municipal Doutor Borges de Medeiros, made several friends and always did well at school. Then he moved to Escola Municipal Arsênio Heiss and did only the fifth grade there, meeting even more people and drifting away from others. Most of his second stage of elementary school he studied at Colégio Adventista de Toledo, which was where he evolved the most mentally and physically through the sports he practiced, drifted away from people he realized were not good for his journey, and then joined the Federal Institute, which is where he knew he would build the best possible future.

Municipality: Toledo - PR

  • History and Geography: Created in the 1950s through the settlement promoted by immigrants mostly from the states of Rio Grande do Sul and Santa Catarina, the municipality stands out in western Paraná as one of the most relevant exponents in food production and the national agricultural sector.
  • Culture and Curiosities: Toledo has national recognition with the title of "Capital of Agribusiness" and is famous for holding the traditional National Roasted Pork Festival. The city also houses the Diva Paim Barth Ecological Park in its central area, consolidated as one of the main entertainment areas for its inhabitants.

Neighborhood: Jardim Gisela

  • Local History: Jardim Gisela established itself as one of the pioneering and most traditional neighborhoods in the urban growth of the municipality. Born from the residential expansion of the city in recent decades, the neighborhood followed Toledo's development while maintaining a welcoming, family-oriented and strongly community-based profile.
  • Characteristics and Remarkable Aspects: Jardim Gisela is a predominantly residential, tree-lined area with excellent infrastructure in Toledo. It stands out for housing important educational institutions, such as the Pontifical Catholic University of Paraná (PUCPR - Toledo Campus), in addition to having a structured local commerce, a variety of services and quick access to the city center and the Diva Paim Barth Ecological Park.
