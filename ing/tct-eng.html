TCTs in Toledo - PR: Rights of the Child and Adolescent

In the municipality of Toledo (PR), the Contemporary Cross-Cutting Theme (TCT) focused on the Rights of the Child and Adolescent — belonging to the macro-area of Citizenship and Civics — is structured based on the integration between social assistance, education, health and the System of Guarantee of Rights. The city stands out for offering a continuous network of protection, community coexistence and social development aimed at early childhood, children and adolescents.

Main Local Actions and Programs

  • Florir Toledo Program: Project aimed at adolescents aged 13 to 17 in situations of social vulnerability. It integrates environmental training, cultural activities, pedagogical follow-up, strengthening of family bonds and the granting of a financial aid scholarship.
  • Coexistence and Bond Strengthening Services (SCFV / Social Units): They offer artistic, sports and recreational activities outside school hours in various neighborhoods, acting directly in the prevention of social risks.
  • Municipal Plan for Early Childhood and Recognitions: Toledo adopts continuous ten-year guidelines and plans to guarantee the rights of the Statute of the Child and Adolescent (ECA), being a reference in social investment and child protection.

Protection Network and Social Control

The execution and monitoring of public policies in the municipality rely on decisive bodies:

  1. CMDCA (Municipal Council for the Rights of the Child and Adolescent): Responsible for deliberating social policies, managing the Municipal Childhood Fund (FIA) and supporting governmental and non-governmental entities.
  2. Guardianship Councils (Units I and II): They act directly in guaranteeing rights and handling reports of violations at the municipal seat and districts.
  3. Permanent Awareness Campaigns: Actions such as the Faça Bonito Campaign (combating abuse and sexual exploitation) with mobilizations in squares, parks and schools.
