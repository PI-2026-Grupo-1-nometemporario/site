MEMBER: Rafael Bremm Meyer

Birth (from a family member's perspective):

"I will never forget that early morning of fine rain on May 14, 2011. When I held Rafael in my arms for the first time at the maternity ward, his eyes were wide open, as if he already wanted to understand everything happening around him. Since he was little, my son was always a calm boy, but extremely curious — he preferred to take apart his own toys to see how they worked inside rather than simply play with them. Watching this little boy grow up and become such a dedicated young man is one of the greatest prides of my life."

— Testimony from Maria Helena Bremm Meyer, Rafael's mother.

Municipality: Toledo - PR

  • History and Geography: Founded in the 1950s by settlers coming mainly from Rio Grande do Sul and Santa Catarina, Toledo stands out in the western region of Paraná as one of the largest hubs of agribusiness and food production in the country.
  • Culture and Curiosities: Toledo is nationally known as the "Capital of Agribusiness" and stands out for the traditional National Roasted Pork Festival. In addition, it has the Diva Paim Barth Ecological Park in the city center, one of the main leisure spots for the population.

Neighborhood: Vila Industrial

  • Characteristics and Remarkable Aspects: Vila Industrial is one of the most populous, dynamic and traditional neighborhoods in Toledo. It stands out for being a region with a strong presence of local commerce, industries, services and for housing important sports and leisure structures in the municipality.
  • Local History: The neighborhood played a fundamental role in the economic development of the municipality, emerging to house the workers and the first productive structures of the city. Over the decades, it transformed into a complete and vibrant community, keeping alive the hardworking and welcoming essence of Toledo.
